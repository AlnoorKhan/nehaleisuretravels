Meet Neha — Your Personal Travel Planner ❤️

Hi, I'm Neha, the founder of Neha's Leisure Travels.

For me, travel is not just about visiting a destination. It is about the people you travel with, the experiences you collect and the memories you bring back home.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to plan customised holidays for families, couples, honeymooners, friends and groups travelling across India and internationally.
